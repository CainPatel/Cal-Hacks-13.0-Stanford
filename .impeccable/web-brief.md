Scope: web/ dashboard, three views (Pay requests with detail, Approve, Security). Mode: Operate.
Audience/job: office manager reviewing this month's pay requests; owner approving escalations; judges on Security.
Constraints: spec palette (light warm neutral, one accent; green pass, amber hold/escalate, red fail, gray pending; color carries nothing else), Geist Sans/Mono, big type and targets, phone-ready, dark only on Security.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A month of pay requests read as one decision list, each line a plain sentence with its proof attached. Refuses the KPI-tile dashboard with a data grid.
OWN-WORLD: Warm stone ground (#f4f2ee), white sheets, ink #1c1a17, one blueprint-blue accent for actions and selection only; status green/amber/red/gray as chips with icon + word. Geist Sans throughout, Geist Mono for money and traces. Hairline rules, 8px radius, no cards-in-cards.
STORY: The office manager sees what's ready, what needs the owner, what's held and why; taps a hold, sees the evidence and the next step; the owner approves on the device; judges see how often it can be robbed.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar (Draw, month, tabs). One summary sentence. Jobs as sections; rows: sub, amount (mono, right), status chip, sentence. Desktop: detail opens in a right pane; phone: full-screen sheet.
FORM: Pinned brief (spec), code-led, signature move: evidence strip beside each hold (claimed vs video tolerance bar, domain diff with the fake letter highlighted, unreleased supplier). Seed key: none (brief-pinned).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
